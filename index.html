NAADA • Instrument Guide

An expressive camera-controlled synthesis instrument uniting Indian Classical Music (Swaras, Thaats, Meend, Tanpura) with computer vision and Web Audio.

Left Hand: Swara & Mudra Selection

1 Finger (Index): Sa (सा) - The root tonic
2 Fingers (Index + Middle): Re (रे)
3 Fingers (Index + Middle + Ring): Ga (ग)
4 Fingers (Index, Middle, Ring, Pinky): Ma (म)
5 Fingers (Open Palm): Pa (प) - Fifth
Index + Pinky: Dha (ध)
Index + Pinky + Thumb: Ni (नि)

Horizontal Wrist Tilt:
• Neutral / Inward: Shuddha (or Teevra for Ma)
• Outward: Komal (flat) (or Shuddha Ma in Teevra Thaats)
When resting neutral, notes automatically conform to your active Thaat!

Right Hand: Expression, Meend & Saptak

Vertical Height: Controls volume & acoustic breath (Higher = Louder, Lower = Softer).
Horizontal Wrist Tilt: Continuous Meend / Gamaka microtonal pitch glide (±100 cents) for authentic vocal/sitar portamento!
Thumb Extended: Drops to Mandra Saptak (lower octave).
Elevated Hand (above shoulder): Ascends to Tar Saptak (higher octave).

Right Hand Fingers (Voicing & Accompaniment):
• 1 Finger: Solo Melodic Swara with fluid Meend portamento
• 2 Fingers: Swara + Sa drone harmony
• 3 Fingers: Swara + Sa + Pa accompaniment
• 4 Fingers: Swarmandal resonant chord cascade

4-String Tanpura Studio

Synthesizes a continuous, meditative acoustic Tanpura with harmonic Jwari buzz. The 4 visual wires in the deck vibrate whenever each string is plucked.
